HEALTHY FOODS KNOWLEDGE BASE

### Food: Avocado
Avocados are nutrient-rich fruits known for their high content of healthy monounsaturated fats, especially oleic acid. They are also packed with fiber, potassium, and vitamins E, K, B5, B6, and folate. Regular consumption supports heart health, improves cholesterol levels, and helps in nutrient absorption from other foods.

### Food: Oats
Oats are whole grains known for their soluble fiber called beta-glucan, which helps reduce cholesterol and control blood sugar levels. They provide slow-releasing carbohydrates, supporting sustained energy throughout the day. Oats are commonly consumed as oatmeal or added to smoothies and baked goods.

### Food: Blueberries
Blueberries are small, nutrient-dense fruits rich in antioxidants, particularly anthocyanins, which contribute to their deep blue color. These antioxidants help protect cells from damage, reduce inflammation, and improve brain function. Blueberries are often called a “superfood” due to their wide-ranging health benefits.

### Food: Spinach
Spinach is a leafy green vegetable high in vitamins A, C, and K, magnesium, iron, and folate. It supports healthy blood, vision, and immune function. Regular consumption may reduce oxidative stress and lower blood pressure.

### Food: Salmon
Salmon is an oily fish rich in omega-3 fatty acids (EPA and DHA), which are essential for heart and brain health. It is also a great source of high-quality protein, B vitamins, and selenium. Eating salmon regularly helps reduce inflammation and may improve mood and cognitive performance.

### Food: Almonds
Almonds are nutrient-dense nuts that provide healthy fats, protein, fiber, and vitamin E. They help control blood sugar, lower cholesterol, and support heart health. Almonds also promote satiety, making them a great snack for weight management.

### Food: Broccoli
Broccoli is a cruciferous vegetable rich in vitamins C and K, fiber, and sulforaphane — a compound known for its cancer-fighting properties. It supports detoxification, gut health, and immune function.

### Food: Chia Seeds
Chia seeds are tiny but powerful sources of omega-3 fatty acids, fiber, protein, calcium, and antioxidants. When soaked, they form a gel-like texture, aiding digestion and hydration. They help stabilize blood sugar and promote heart health.

### Food: Greek Yogurt
Greek yogurt is a high-protein dairy product that contains probiotics beneficial for gut health. It supports digestion, strengthens bones due to its calcium content, and helps with muscle recovery after exercise.

### Food: Sweet Potatoes
Sweet potatoes are rich in complex carbohydrates, fiber, and beta-carotene (a precursor to vitamin A). They provide a steady source of energy and support vision, immune function, and gut health.

### Food: Quinoa
Quinoa is a gluten-free pseudo-grain high in protein and essential amino acids. It’s also rich in magnesium, iron, and fiber, supporting energy metabolism, muscle repair, and digestive health.

### Food: Garlic
Garlic contains sulfur compounds, such as allicin, which have antibacterial, antiviral, and anti-inflammatory properties. It supports heart health by reducing cholesterol and blood pressure levels.

### Food: Green Tea
Green tea is a powerful source of antioxidants known as catechins, particularly EGCG. Regular consumption supports metabolism, brain function, and cardiovascular health while reducing oxidative stress.

### Food: Eggs
Eggs are nutrient-dense, providing high-quality protein, choline, and vitamins B12, D, and selenium. They support brain development, eye health, and muscle repair.

### Food: Lentils
Lentils are legumes rich in protein, fiber, iron, and folate. They promote digestive health, help regulate blood sugar, and are an excellent plant-based protein source.

### Food: Apples
Apples are rich in fiber, vitamin C, and various antioxidants. They support digestion, promote satiety, and may lower the risk of heart disease.

### Food: Turmeric
Turmeric contains curcumin, a bioactive compound with potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It supports joint health, boosts brain function, and may reduce the risk of chronic diseases.

### Food: Kale
Kale is a nutrient powerhouse, loaded with vitamins A, K, C, and calcium. It contains antioxidants and compounds that support liver detoxification and heart health.

### Food: Dark Chocolate (70%+ Cocoa)
Dark chocolate with high cocoa content is rich in flavonoids that improve blood flow, reduce inflammation, and enhance mood. It supports heart and brain health when consumed in moderation.

### Food: Flaxseeds
Flaxseeds are rich in omega-3s, lignans, and fiber. They promote digestive health, support hormonal balance, and may help lower cholesterol levels.
